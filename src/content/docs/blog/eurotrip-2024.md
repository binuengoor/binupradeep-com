First time in Europe, and I had a movie playing in my head: golden light, cobbled lanes, a violin somewhere, everything dreamy and perfectly in order. That movie lasted about forty minutes in Rome. The real thing turned out to be better.

<!-- excerpt -->

Eleven days, two countries, no rental car: Rome and the Vatican, a sunset in Florence, a train day that went spectacularly off-plan, the Swiss Alps around Interlaken, and one last evening walk through Milan.

This one has its own page, with postcards, polaroids, a departure board and a field guide to supplì.
